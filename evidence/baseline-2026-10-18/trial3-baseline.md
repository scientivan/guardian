## Key reasons the bill exceeds the estimate  

1. **Longer hospital stay (5 days vs. 3 days)** – every day adds room, nursing, doctor rounds, meals, and medication costs.  
2. **Antibiotic course extended** – the physician decided to continue IV antibiotics for five days to prevent post‑operative infection. This adds both drug costs and the daily nursing/ward‑round fees.  
3. **Consumables are charged “as used”** – the estimate explicitly said consumables were *not* included. The bill lists every item (gauze, gloves, IV lines, catheters, etc.). In a real operation these can easily run into a few hundred yuan.  
4. **Detailed lab and imaging work‑up** – the estimate gave a single “laboratory & imaging” line. The actual bill shows the exact tests that were performed, many of which are required to monitor for infection, kidney/liver function, and electrolyte balance after surgery.  
5. **Separate line items for surgery components** – the estimate bundled the operation, anaesthesia and theatre use into one figure (CNY 25 000). The hospital’s billing system separates them, so you see a lower surgery charge (CNY 18 000) but additional anaesthesia (CNY 4 500) and theatre (CNY 2 500) fees.  
6. **Additional administrative fees** – copy of medical record, etc., are small but not part of the original estimate.
