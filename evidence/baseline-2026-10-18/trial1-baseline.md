## What to tell the family

2. **Your father’s stay was 5 days**, because the surgeon decided to keep the antibiotics for 5 days to prevent infection. The extra 2 days automatically add the daily bed, nursing and doctor‑round fees.  
3. **All “consumables” (IV lines, dressings, gloves, catheters, etc.) are charged per item**. The estimate warned that these would be billed as used, and the bill shows the exact count.

5. **Laboratory & imaging tests were performed more than the “baseline”** (e.g., two CBCs, a CRP, full metabolic panels, ultrasound, X‑ray). These are necessary for safe post‑op monitoring and are itemised.  
6. **Small administrative and comfort items** (meal charge, medical‑record copy, pillow‑case, bed‑sheet changes, etc.) are not part of the high‑level estimate but appear on the final invoice.
